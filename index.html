How to Play
  • Each player is secretly assigned one Tiki.
  • The 8 tikis form a totem pole in random order.
  • On your turn, play one card from your hand of 5.
  • ↑ Move Up — advance a specific tiki one step up.
  • ↓ Move Down — push a specific tiki one step down.
  • 💥 Topple — remove the tiki at the very top forever.
  • After playing, you draw one replacement card.
  • The game ends after 4 topples.
  • The player whose tiki sits highest in the remaining pole wins!
